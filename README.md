# Compaign-Analysis-BCGX
## NewCo Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis
### Goal & Scope:
How do you know which marketing message actually moves the needle for first-time buyers? That was the core challenge for NewCo after a one-week, multi-channel marketing trial.
<img width="260" height="150" alt="BCG_X" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/af314c09-660b-4284-851a-e2276be48130" />
The goal of this project was to dive into the raw performance data of two distinct messaging strategies:
* **Campaign A: A conversational, informal tone.**
* **Campaign B: A direct, sales-focused promotional tone.**

The scope involved cleaning raw interaction data, **building dynamic pivot tables** to segment performance, and creating visual dashboards to spotlight conversion trends.

### Outcomes :
Numbers tell a story, and the data for NewCo revealed a major shift in behavior between new and returning customers:
<img width="1000" height="600" alt="Code_Generated_Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/de0e72c6-7cfc-47c4-830e-ad109d028649" />

**The Power of Tone:** While a promotional "hard sell" worked for loyal customers, it backfired with new audiences. Campaign A (Informal) was the clear winner for acquisition, outperforming the promotional alternative by 76% in total revenue among new users.

**Email is Still King:** Regardless of the tone, Email remained the most efficient channel for conversions, though Instagram showed significant promise when paired with the right informal messaging.

**The Bottom Line:** By isolating the "New Customer" segment using custom slicers and visual trend analysis, uncovered that the informal, trust-building approach of Campaign A generated $3,727 in new sales, compared to just $2,117 from the promotional version.
